Player Rules

📝

Registration

Maximum 16 players can participate in the tournament.

🏟️

Group Stage

4 groups will contain 4 players each. Every player plays 3 group matches.

🏆

Qualification

Top 2 players from each group qualify for the Quarter Final.

⏱️

Match Duration

Regular matches will be 8 minutes.

🔥

Knockout

Draw in knockout matches will go to Extra Time and Penalty Shootout.

👑

Final

The Final match will be 12 minutes.

📸

Screenshot

Match result screenshot must be submitted after every match.

⚡

Fair Play

Cheat, hack, exploit or unfair methods are not allowed.

🤝

Respect

Players must behave respectfully with opponents and organizers.

🆔

eFootball ID

The same eFootball ID must be used throughout the tournament.
