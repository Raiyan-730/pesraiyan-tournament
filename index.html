Rules & Guidelines

Tournament guidelines

  • Every player must use their registered eFootball ID.
  • Each match will be played for 8 minutes.
  • Match condition will be Random.
  • Group Stage matches will be played without Extra Time.
  • Group Stage matches will be played without Penalty Shootout.
  • Players must complete their matches within the deadline.
  • Match scores must be submitted correctly.
  • Fake or edited results are not allowed.
  • Respect all players and maintain fair play.
  • In the Knockout Stage, Extra Time will be enabled.
  • In the Knockout Stage, Penalty Shootout will be enabled if required.
  • The tournament organizer has the final decision in disputes.
